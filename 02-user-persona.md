# User Persona
## Inuit Chatbot

### Persona: "The Curious Browser" — Primary persona

- **Who they are:** Landed on Inuit's website via search, social ad, or word of mouth. Interested in premium footwear but not yet committed to a specific pair.
- **Mindset:** "I like the idea of a luxury shoe, but the site has a lot going on — I don't want to dig through menus."
- **Motivations:** Wants to feel confident the shoes are genuinely well-made (craftsmanship matters at this price point), and wants a fast, low-effort way to find something that matches their taste.
- **Frustrations:** Generic e-commerce chat popups that just say "How can I help?" and go nowhere; long forms; not knowing where to click next.
- **What makes them stay:** A conversation that feels personal, gets to the point quickly, and shows (not just tells) why the product is worth the price — hence the "how it's made" videos.
- **What makes them convert:** Feeling like their specific preference was heard, and a low-friction path straight to checkout once they're interested.

### Secondary persona: "The Gift Shopper"

- Browsing for someone else, less sure of exact sizing/style — needs the bot to keep options broad early (hence starting with general preference questions, not exact SKU-level detail).

### Design implications for the bot

| Persona trait | Bot design response |
|---|---|
| Low patience for menus/forms | Use Quick Replies and Buttons instead of free-text wherever possible |
| Needs reassurance on craftsmanship | Dedicate a clear, unskippable-but-not-annoying moment to the "how it's made" videos |
| Wants to feel heard | Reflect their choice back before moving to the next step (e.g., "Great pick — here's what we've got in [category]") |
| May not convert same visit | Order-placement CTA should be easy to find again from any point (via a persistent "Shop now" style button), not just at the very end |
