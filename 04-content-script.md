# Content & Script Document
## Inuit Chatbot — Every Line the Bot Says

Tone guide: warm, concise, a little playful — like a well-trained boutique store assistant, not a call-center script. Short sentences. One emoji per message max, used purposefully (never decorative spam).

---

### Step 1 — Welcome
> 👋 Hey there! Welcome to Inuit — handcrafted, premium shoes made to last a lifetime (and look good doing it).
>
> I'm here to help you find your pair. Want a quick tour?

**Quick Replies:** `Yes, show me around` | `I know what I want` | `Just browsing`

---

### Step 2 — Preference Discovery

**2a.**
> Nice! Who are we shopping for today?

**Quick Replies:** `Myself` | `Someone else` | `⬅ Back`

**2b.**
> Got it. And what's the vibe — everyday comfort, something formal, or classic sneakers?

**Quick Replies:** `Everyday` | `Formal` | `Sneakers` | `Not sure yet` | `⬅ Back`

**Reflection line (after answer):**
> [Formal] it is 🖤 — let's see what we've got.

---

### Step 3 — Curated Picks (Carousel)
> Here are a few pieces I think you'll love:

**Carousel cards (3):**
- Card 1: *The Marchetti Oxford* — ₹18,999 — `View`
- Card 2: *The Bellwood Loafer* — ₹15,499 — `View`
- Card 3: *The Ashford Derby* — ₹21,999 — `View`

**After carousel:**
> Want to see how these are actually made? It's kind of our favorite part.

**Buttons:** `Show me 🎥` | `Skip to ordering` | `⬅ Back`

---

### Step 4 — Craftsmanship Videos (Carousel)
> Every Inuit shoe takes about 18 hours of handwork. Here's a peek behind the curtain:

**Carousel cards (3):**
- Card 1: *From Leather to Last: Sourcing* — `Watch`
- Card 2: *Handstitched: Inside Our Workshop* — `Watch`
- Card 3: *Final Polish: Quality Check* — `Watch`

**After watching / skipping:**
> Pretty cool, right? So — ready to make one of these yours?

**Buttons:** `Yes, let's order 🛍` | `Show me more shoes` | `⬅ Back`

---

### Step 5 — Order Prompt
> Great choice — *[shoe name]* it is. Let's get it to your door.
>
> Could I get your delivery pincode?

*(free-text expected here — one of very few free-text moments in the flow)*

**After valid pincode:**
> Perfect, we deliver there in 3–5 days. Shall I confirm the order?

**Buttons:** `Confirm order ✅` | `Change my pick` | `⬅ Back`

---

### Step 6 — Closing
> 🎉 You're all set! Order confirmed — you'll get a tracking link by email shortly.
>
> Anything else I can help with?

**Buttons:** `Track my order` | `Talk to support` | `No thanks, bye 👋`

**If "bye":**
> Thanks for stopping by Inuit — hope to see you (and your new shoes) again soon! 👋
